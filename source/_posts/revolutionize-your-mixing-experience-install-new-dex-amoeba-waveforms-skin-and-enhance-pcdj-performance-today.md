**Skin Designer and PCDJ aficionado DJ Vargas has planted himself behind Photoshop once again and created another brilliant [DEX 3 DJ Mixing Software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) Skin for our community.** 

[![](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/dex3skin-new.jpg?fit=300%2C186&ssl=1 "dex3skin-new")](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/dex3skin-new.jpg?fit=1030%2C639&ssl=1)

DJ Vargas, AKA Emiliano Vargas, is a pro DJ from Sweden with knack for design. [Here’s one of his previous DEX 3 Denon DJ skin submissions](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/). His new high-resolution 4-deck **_“Vertical Wave”_** DEX 3 skin submission includes 4 vertical waveform’s representing each independent deck, which lends itself nicely to both beat-mixing and scratching. If you like to utilize all 4 DJ decks when you mix you’ll want to download and try the new skin.

Emiliano created the new DEX 3 DJ software skin utilizing our latest edition of our free PCDJ skin designer software. The latest [DEX 3.2](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) updated graphics engine supports user-created skins utilizing our new and improved “**[PCDJ Skin Designer](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)**.” This allows DEX 3 users to change the entire look and feel of the GUI ([_graphical user interface_](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphical%5Fuser%5Finterface)).
